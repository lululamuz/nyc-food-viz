About the data

Source: NYCFoodInspectionSimple, NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, downloaded October 11, 2026. The raw file has one row per inspection or violation (about 297,000 rows), so each restaurant was counted only once (same name, street and ZIP code). Restaurants with no cuisine listed were left out. The chart shows the 10 most common cuisines in each borough, and every other cuisine is grouped into the last slice.
